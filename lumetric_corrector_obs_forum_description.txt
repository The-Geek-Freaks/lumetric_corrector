[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#9370DB]Description[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[QUOTE]
[COLOR=#8A6BBE][SIZE=4]Lumetric Corrector adds a complete color correction and grading pipeline to OBS Studio: tone controls, white balance, color wheels, split toning, creative effects, vignette, masking and 3D LUTs – all in a single GPU pass, controlled from the normal filter dialog.[/SIZE][/COLOR]
[/QUOTE]

[SIZE=6][B][COLOR=#9370DB]⚡ Main Features[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]

[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#8A6BBE]Color Correction[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Tone:[/B] Exposure, Contrast, Brightness, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks – luminance-weighted, so tone changes do not shift hues
[*][B]Fade & Lift:[/B] Highlight Fade, Shadow Fade, Black Lift for matte and bleach film looks
[*][B]White Balance & Color:[/B] Temperature, Tint, Saturation, Vibrance
[/LIST]

[CENTER][IMG]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/The-Geek-Freaks/lumetric_corrector/refs/heads/main/docs/features-correction.png[/IMG]
[SIZE=3][I]Color correction controls[/I][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#8A6BBE]Color Grading[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Color wheels:[/B] separate RGB offsets for shadows, midtones and highlights (Lift / Gamma / Gain)
[*][B]Split toning:[/B] independent color and strength for shadows and highlights
[*][B]3D LUTs:[/B] load .cube files of any size or .png LUTs (OBS 512x512 grid) with adjustable strength – Windows, macOS and Linux
[/LIST]

[CENTER][IMG]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/The-Geek-Freaks/lumetric_corrector/refs/heads/main/docs/features-grading.png[/IMG]
[SIZE=3][I]Grading tools[/I][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[SIZE=5][B][COLOR=#8A6BBE]Creative Effects & Workflow[/COLOR][/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Bloom, Halation, Sharpen, animated Film Grain, Vignette[/B] with radius, feather and shape
[*][B]Mask:[/B] apply the whole grade only inside an ellipse or rectangle with soft edges
[*][B]47 presets[/B] (Basic, Style, Mood, Era, Creative) that apply instantly, plus "Reset all"
[*][B]Looks:[/B] save and load all color settings as JSON to share between scenes, profiles and computers
[*][B]LUT export:[/B] bake your look into a 33³ .cube or an OBS-compatible 512x512 .png LUT
[/LIST]
